The Three Levels of "Life Without Oxygen" (Table 1)

Scientific discussions often conflate organismal oxygen gas dependence with molecular oxygen atom composition. Our framework introduces a formal three-tier boundary:

Level 1 - Established
No Molecular Oxygen ($O_2$) Required

Anaerobic fermentation and respiration conserve metabolic energy without $O_2$ gas.

Level 2 - Established
Oxygen-Bearing Alternative Acceptors

Respiration using nitrate ($NO_3^-$), sulfate ($SO_4^{2-}$), or $CO_2$. Organisms still deeply rely on oxygen atoms.

Level 3 - Speculative
Zero Oxygen Atoms in Molecular Makeup

Hypothetical systems built without any oxygen atoms. Undemonstrated in nature; strictly speculative.
